Tip Sheet:

Refusing Signatures and Rerouting to correct person for esigning

If a signature was routed to you by mistake, you can refuse to sign and send to correct person.

When logging into HCS, your patient worklist pops up.

  1. Select the patient from your work list.
  2. Select the document you want to refuse due to sent by mistake and needs to be routed to correct person.
  3. Click “Refuse to Sign”.

  1. A popup message will come up, click “Okay”.

  1. A Notes box will popup, you will enter why you are refusing to sign and click “Okay”.

  1. Your patient work list will now have your comment that you refused and why you refused

  1. From your patient work list, double click on the document you just refused, the form will open and you will then reroute to the correct person to sign.
    1. Clear where your signature is pending

    1. Route to correct person, click “Use Signature”.

    1. Click save
