PROVIDER DISCHARGE PROCESS

HCS PROVIDER DISCHARGE

HCS – HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS

Phone: 334-279-9711

HCSSupport@hcsinc.net

STEPS:

  • Complete paper discharge per facility process, excluding medication reconciliation.
  • Locate patient in HCS
  • Verify preferred pharmacy if desire to ePrescribe
  • Select Discharge Tab
  • Mark each medication as Rx, Continue or Stop
  • Select eSign or eSign and Send as appropriate

UPDATING PREFERRED PHARMACY

  • In the “Preferred Pharmacy” box, begin entering Name, City, State or ZIP to start search.
    • Can search “Walgreens in Springfield” or “CVS in 36117”. Make selection from results.

*Any pharmacy that produced results from the query will show as an option.

COLUMNS/COLORS/CODES

  • Status Column – will show origination of medication order
    • H (Red)– Patient was taking this medication at home, but it was not continued in the hospital
    • H-MQ (Red) – Medication came from Med query but was not reconciled at admission. (*This is a workflow problem)
    • I (Green) – Inpatient
    • B (Blue) – Home medication continued the saw way in the hospital
    • H, I, B (Purple) – Linked medications, they are alike but something is different. Be aware when reconciling.
  • RX Column - will show if prescriptions were indicated
  • E-RX Column -
    • Icons and codes formulary status, copay information whether or not a medication is OTC, and whether the prescription has been sent. The column displays a question mark (?) if the formulary status is unknown
  • After the prescription is sent, the insurance statuses disappear and are replaced by e-prescription statuses

TO ePRESCRIBE

  • Provider must have ePrescribe access
  • Patient must have preferred pharmacy listed
  • All medications must be addressed with RX, Continue or Stop

AFTER SELECTIONS ARE MADE

  • An Rx Send Summary window is displayed, allowing final check and confirmation of prescriptions to be eprescribed

  • Send e-Rxs button to complete the process
