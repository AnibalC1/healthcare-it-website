### Hero

**Headline:** "Free IT Security Assessment for Your Practice"
**Subheadline:** "Find out exactly where your practice stands on security, compliance, and disaster readiness — at no cost."

### What We Review

1. **Network Security** — Firewall configuration, Wi-Fi security, access controls
2. **HIPAA Compliance** — Technical safeguard gaps, risk analysis status, documentation
3. **Backup & Disaster Recovery** — Backup verification, recovery time estimates, offsite copies
4. **Equipment Health** — Hardware age, warranty status, replacement planning
5. **Patient Data Protection** — Encryption status, access logging, breach readiness

### What You Get

- A written report with findings and prioritized recommendations
- Risk score for your current IT infrastructure
- HIPAA compliance gap summary
- Estimated costs to address critical issues
- No obligation — the report is yours to keep regardless

### Why It Is Free

> "We believe every healthcare practice deserves to know where they stand. Most practices we assess have at least 2-3 critical gaps they were not aware of. By showing you the reality, we earn your trust — and many practices choose to work with us after seeing the results."

### FAQ

**Q: How long does it take?**
A: About 1 hour on-site. We will have your report within 48 hours.

**Q: Do I need to prepare anything?**
A: Just make sure we can access your server room/closet and a workstation. We handle the rest.

**Q: Is there really no obligation?**
A: None. The assessment and report are free. If you want help fixing what we find, we will provide a quote. If not, keep the report.

**Q: Will this disrupt my practice?**
A: No. We work quietly and do not need to take any systems offline.
